Fieldwork

In the field since 2019

Field campaigns from the Sundarbans to the northern plains, and participatory work with the communities who live there.

Fieldwork

Methods used in the field include participatory rural appraisal (PRA), key informant interviews, household surveys, beach profiling, river cross-sections and vegetation sampling.

  1. 2024–
    13 districts of north-west Bangladesh
    Participatory rural appraisal for aquifer mapping and water stress assessment (WARPO).
  2. 2024
    Sylhet region
    Master's field survey of socioeconomic dynamics, microclimate and environmental profile, including surveys with tea workers at Khadimnagar Tea Estate, now being written up as a journal article.
  3. 2023
    Sundarbans · Jamtola Sea Beach
    Climatic variables and plant community patterns using the Braun-Blanquet cover-abundance scale.
  4. 2022
    Kuakata Sea Beach, Barishal
    SWOT analysis of a coastal tourism site.
  5. 2020
    Bandarban, Teknaf & Saint Martin's Island
    Land use, slope analysis, beach profiling and vegetation survey, plus an economic survey with interviews on Saint Martin's Island.
  6. 2019
    Northern Bangladesh · Dinajpur
    River cross-section profiling, soil texture analysis, and interviews and a socio-economic survey with the Santal community.
