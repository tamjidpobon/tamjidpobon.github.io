Floods · Article in Bangla, 2024

Fifty years of floods in Bangladesh

With Bibi Hafsa and Jannatun Hussna Tuya, I reviewed the major floods between 1970 and 2020, mapped the area each one covered, and combined the record into maps of flood-prone districts and flood-prone land. Published in সমাজবিজ্ঞান সমীক্ষা (Social Science Review), Jahangirnagar University, issue 7, July 2024.
