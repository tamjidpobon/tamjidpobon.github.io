LegendAbout

Spatial analysis for climate hazards and the people who live with them.

I work where Earth observation meets community research. I measure how extreme events change the land, from shoreline erosion after a cyclone to shifts in vegetation, and I pair that with participatory fieldwork to understand risk, vulnerability and adaptation on the ground.

Since 2024 I have applied the same methods in practice at the Center for Environmental and Geographic Information Services (CEGIS), on national projects ranging from groundwater stress to the Economic Census.

  • Risk, vulnerability and community

    Community resilience, adaptation, participatory research, flood risk, and hazard-related mobility and evacuation.

  • Change during extreme events

    Tropical cyclone impacts, shoreline erosion and deposition, vegetation change, coastal and fluvial geomorphology, sediment dynamics.

  • Methods

    GIS, remote sensing, Earth observation and spatial modelling, combined with participatory and survey-based field methods.

Pobon, M. T. A. N., Akter, R., and Mahmud, K. H. Submitted to The Jahangirnagar Review. Preprint on Research Square, November 2025 · doi:10.21203/rs.3.rs-7954742/v1

MSc thesis

Geo-spatial Technology for Rapid Eco-Physical Impact Assessment of Tropical Cyclones of the Bay of Bengal

  • Measured the physical impact of multiple cyclones with multi-temporal GIS and remote sensing, using shoreline erosion and deposition as the main indicator alongside vegetation change.
  • Quantified shoreline change for four cyclones (Sitrang, Bulbul, Amphan and Mocha) using Landsat 8 imagery, MNDWI and the Digital Shoreline Analysis System (DSAS).
  • Compared impacts across cyclone categories to gauge relative severity.
  • Zoned coastal districts by impact, giving a spatial framework for risk management.
BSc dissertation

GIS-based Multi-Criteria Approach for Flood Susceptibility Mapping of Bangladesh

  • Compiled an inventory of flood hazards and mapped the key flood conditioning factors.
  • Built a multi-criteria susceptibility model to support risk assessment and management.
